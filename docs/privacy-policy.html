Privacy Policy

Habits for iPhone · Effective 1 October 2026

In short: Habits stores everything on your device. We don't collect, share, or send any of your data to any server. Subscriptions are handled by Apple.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following information locally on your iPhone:

  • The habits you create, including names, categories, goals, units, schedules and reminder settings.
  • What you log for each habit, such as check-offs, amounts and minutes, and any running timer.
  • Your app preferences, such as list or grid view and custom categories.

This information never leaves your device through the app. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Data we collect

None. Habits has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party services. The only time the app goes online is to talk to Apple's App Store about your subscription.

Subscription

Habits is paid for with a subscription bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only whether a subscription is active. We never see your name, Apple Account, payment details or purchase history. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Data we share

None. Because we don't collect any data, there is nothing to share, sell, or transfer.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, your device's backup may include the app's data. Those backups are handled by Apple under your own settings and Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to them.

Deleting your data

You can edit or remove habits in the app at any time. Deleting the app from your iPhone permanently removes all of its data from the device.

Children

Habits does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before any new behavior ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub issues page.

Habits for iPhone

Need help? Most answers are below. For anything else, report a problem or ask a question on the GitHub issues page.

Getting started

The app has a built-in guide to every feature. Open it from Settings → How Habits works.

Common questions

How do I move my habits to a new iPhone?

On your old iPhone, go to Settings → Back up data and save the file to Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop it. On the new iPhone, open Habits, go to Settings → Restore from backup and pick that file. Restoring replaces any habits already on the new phone.

Setting up a new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup also brings your habits along.

Is my data synced between devices?

No. Everything stays on the iPhone you use the app on, and nothing is uploaded. Use a backup file to move it, as above.

I already subscribed. How do I get access on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

In the app, go to Settings → Subscription. You can also manage it in the iPhone Settings app under your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check Settings → Notifications in the app, and that notifications for Habits are allowed in the iPhone Settings app. Habit reminders are only sent while the habit is still open that day.

Why don't I see time-of-day stats?

They're hidden when Settings → Time-of-day stats is off, which suits people who log all their habits at once. Turn it on if you tick habits off as you do them.

How do I get my data out?

Settings → Export as spreadsheet creates a CSV file you can open in Numbers or Excel.
